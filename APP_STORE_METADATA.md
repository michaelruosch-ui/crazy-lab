## App-Review-Hinweis (Entwurf, Englisch)

Crazy Lab is a Kids Category app for ages 9–11. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking,
accounts or cloud synchronization. Profiles, diary entries, photos and videos stay locally on the
device. External actions, profile deletion, purchase and restore are protected by a native device
authentication parental gate. The app offers six complete free missions and one non-consumable
in-app purchase (`ch.crazylab.fullversion`) that permanently unlocks all other missions. Reviewers
can use the supplied StoreKit/App Store sandbox product; no separate login is required.
